GDG stands for Google Developer Groups—a global network of local, community-led chapters for developers passionate about Google's ecosystem and modern software development.

01 / A Home for Builders

Locally organized.
Globally empowered.

Google Developer Groups are community groups for developers interested in Google developer technology: Android, Chrome, Drive, and Google Cloud platforms.

⚡ Powered by Google Tech 3 / 7

Yes, you got it right—we are crazy about Google technologies!

Google supports our community by providing:

Sponsorship & Swag

Event funding, infrastructure, and official gear.

Developer Ecosystem

Direct access to platforms like Android, Google Cloud, Firebase, and Gemini.

Industry Experts

Connections to Google Developer Experts (GDEs) and Googler speakers.

Ecosystem: Google Cloud Gemini AI Android Flutter Firebase

Run by the Community, for the Community 👥

While supported by Google, we are not paid Google employees or official corporate representatives.

We are local software engineers, students, and tech enthusiasts donating our time to share knowledge, exchange ideas, and build local tech ecosystems worldwide.

You Are the Main Power of GDG

The true force behind this global movement isn't code, APIs, or platforms—it is people. Community initiatives and flagship events—like DevFests, Study Jams, and Hackathons—happen because passionate individuals step up to build something bigger than themselves. By joining us, you aren't just helping run an event; you are shaping the local developer ecosystem and inspiring the next generation of builders.

Join our GDG team as a volunteer and help us spread knowledge, spark innovation, and share the passion!
